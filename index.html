Jiwon Jeon

I'm a Ph.D. student in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, where I work with Prof. YoungChul Sung in the Smart Information Systems Research Lab (SISReL). My research focuses on LLM reasoning and multi-agent reinforcement learning, and my recent interest is in LLM harnessing. I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST, and was a research intern at LG AI Research (2023–2024).
